# Sleep Sounds recordings

All are CC0 (public domain) field recordings from Freesound, used with no conditions.
Cut into seamless loops and level-matched by `Marketing/sleep-sounds-candidates/make-loops.sh` in the app repo.
The heartbeat loop is 55 whole beats (0.98 s each) with a 0.3 s blend in a quiet gap.
